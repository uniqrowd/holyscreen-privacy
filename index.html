Privacy Policy

Holy Screen does not collect or transmit any personal information to external servers.
All data stays on your device, and the app works completely offline.

1 Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information is involved when you use Holy Screen (the "App") and how it is handled.

The developer respects your privacy and complies with applicable data protection laws.

  • App name: Holy Screen
  • Package ID: com.holyscreen.daily
  • Effective date: May 1, 2026

2 Information We Collect None

Holy Screen does not collect any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email address, phone number, etc.)
  • Location data
  • Camera, microphone, or contacts access
  • Advertising identifiers (ADID / IDFA)
  • Usage statistics or analytics data

3 Data Stored on Your Device

To provide its features, the App stores the following data locally on your device only. This data is never transmitted externally.

  • Your Bible verses: The scripture text you add for display on the lock screen (stored in Hive DB, on-device only)
  • App settings: Font size, background color, verse rotation interval, etc. (stored in SharedPreferences, on-device only)
  • Daily recommendation history: Index numbers of verses shown today — used to prevent same-day duplicates, automatically cleared the next day (SharedPreferences)

4 App Permissions and Their Purpose

  • SET_WALLPAPER: Used solely to apply Bible verse images to the lock screen and home screen wallpaper
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Used only when you choose a photo from your gallery as a background image
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Used to restore lock screen settings automatically after the device restarts
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Used to update the displayed verse at precise intervals (e.g., every 1 minute, 1 hour, or 24 hours) as configured by the user
  • REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS: Used to prevent verse updates from being skipped due to battery-saving mode restrictions

No permission is used for any purpose beyond what is described above.

5 Third-Party Sharing & External Transmission None

Holy Screen contains no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, or tracking.

The App does not use internet permissions and never transmits user data to any external server.

6 Children's Privacy

Holy Screen does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because no personal data is collected at all, the App is safe for users of all ages.

7 Data Deletion

Uninstalling the App from your device permanently deletes all locally stored data (verse list, settings, etc.).

You can also clear app data at any time via Device Settings → Apps → Holy Screen → Storage → Clear Data.

8 Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the changes will be communicated through an app update and the effective date on this page will be revised accordingly.

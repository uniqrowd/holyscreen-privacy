Privacy Policy – HolyScreen

Last updated: June 3, 2026

1. Overview

HolyScreen ("we", "our", or "the app") is a free Android application that displays Bible verses on your lock screen. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email address, phone number, etc.)
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Any data that is transmitted to external servers

All user preferences and custom Bible verses are stored locally on your device only using Android SharedPreferences and local file storage.

3. Permissions Used

  • SET_WALLPAPER / SET_WALLPAPER_HINTS – Required to set the lock screen wallpaper with Bible verse text.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_IMAGES – Required only if you choose a photo from your gallery as a background image. The selected image is copied to the app's private storage and the original is not modified.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED – Used to reschedule the automatic lock screen update after the device restarts.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+) – Used solely for background service notifications required by Android OS for foreground workers.

4. Third-Party Services

HolyScreen does not integrate any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, analytics services, or social media platforms.

5. Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected from any user, the app is safe for all ages.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
